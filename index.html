Our Story

Six Decades of
Trusted Service

Founded in 1963, HSI Solutions has grown into a comprehensive healthcare services organization dedicated to the long-term viability of rural hospitals and clinics across the Northern Plains.

Based in Bismarck, North Dakota, we serve members across North Dakota, South Dakota, Montana, and Minnesota — delivering specialized expertise that small hospitals cannot afford to maintain on their own.
